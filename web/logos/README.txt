Store logos
===========

Put a store's logo here and point to it from scraper/config/stores.yaml:

  - id: hukut
    name: Hukut
    logo: logos/hukut.svg

- SVG is best. PNG or WebP also work: at least 96 x 96 pixels.
- Square or nearly square logos look best. Wide wordmarks get shrunk to fit.
- Use a transparent or white background. Logos are shown on a white tile, in light and dark mode.
- Ask the store for its logo (or permission to use it) before adding it.

A store without a logo, or whose logo file is missing, shows its initials instead.
