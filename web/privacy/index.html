    Privacy

    Last updated 18 November 2026

    Bhaau is an independent website that compares iPhone prices in Nepal. This page explains what we keep about you and why. You can use the whole site without telling us anything. If you sign up for a price alert, we keep your email address only to send that alert.

    Using the website

    • There are no accounts and no sign-in.
    • We don't use advertising or analytics trackers, and we don't set cookies.
    • If you pick the Light or Dark theme, that choice is saved in your own browser. It is never sent to us.
    • Like every website, our hosting provider receives your IP address and browser details when you open a page, and may keep them in its server logs for a short time for security.
    • Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so Google also receives your IP address when a page loads.

    Prices

    Prices, stock and product links come from sellers' public websites. We don't collect any information about sellers' customers.

    Price alerts

    When you create a price alert, we store:

    • your email address
    • the iPhone and storage size you chose, and your target price if you set one
    • when you signed up, confirmed, and were last emailed
    • a scrambled code made from your connection's IP address (not the address itself), used only to stop too many sign-ups from one connection

    We use this only to email you when that iPhone's price drops. We don't send newsletters or advertising, and we never sell or share your email address.

    Nothing is switched on until you click the link in the confirmation email. If you don't click it, your sign-up is deleted after 7 days.

    To stop, use the Stop alerts link at the bottom of any alert email. You can stop one phone or all of them. Stopping deletes the alert straight away; we don't keep a copy.

    Two services handle alerts for us: Cloudflare stores the alert list and Resend sends the emails. Their servers may be outside Nepal, and they use the data only to provide this service to us.

    Your choices

    You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by replying to any email we sent you. The Stop alerts link deletes your alert immediately.

    Changes

    If this page changes, the date at the top changes too.
